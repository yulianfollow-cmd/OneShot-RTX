OneShot RTX (RayTrace2D)
========================

Open the menu:  HOME, F8, or a quick tap of ALT (while walking around).
Move:           arrows or W A S D.   Old A/S/W actions moved to Q/E/R.
Files here:     RayTrace2D.rb     the mod
                RayTrace2D.cfg    your settings (created by the mod)
                RayTrace2D.log    what happened (check it if something is off)
                ru_custom\*.txt   improved Russian lines (built into ru_custom.po)
Uninstall:      run uninstall.bat from the download, or restore
                Data\xScripts.rxdata.rt2d-backup over Data\xScripts.rxdata.
